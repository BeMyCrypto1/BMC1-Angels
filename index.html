Signs your transmission needs attention

Catching these early can mean a smaller repair. If you notice any of them, get it checked.

  • Slipping out of gearRPMs jump but the car doesn't keep up.
  • Delay when shifting into Drive or ReverseA pause, then a clunk.
  • Hard or rough shiftsJerking between gears.
  • Grinding or a stiff clutchCommon in manual transmissions.
  • Red or brown fluid under the carTransmission fluid is usually red.
  • Burning smell or a warning lightDon't keep driving on it.
